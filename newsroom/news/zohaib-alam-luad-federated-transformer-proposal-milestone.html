Research Milestone

LUAD Research Proposal by Zohaib Alam Shortlisted for ICRI-STE R&D Committee Submission

A proposal on privacy-preserving federated transformers for lung adenocarcinoma prediction using genomic DNA sequences has been shortlisted for submission to the ICRI-STE R&D Committee.

20 June 2026·ECR R&D·ICRI-STE

The International Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE) has informed Zohaib Alam that his research proposal, “A Privacy-Preserving Federated Transformer Framework for Lung Adenocarcinoma Prediction Using Genomic DNA Sequences,” has been shortlisted for submission to the ICRI-STE R&D Committee.

The letter dated 20 June 2026 confirms his complimentary membership in the ICRI-STE Future Leaders Network and classification as ECR-1C (BS-CS Completed), based on his BS in Computer Science from the University of Haripur, Pakistan.

Next evaluation stage

According to the correspondence, the proposal is to be submitted to the R&D Committee. Following that stage, it is expected to proceed to the Research Pitching Commission, comprising national and international professors, for further evaluation. The letter states that the researcher will be invited to present a research pitch deck in line with the mission and vision of the ICRI-STE R&D Programmes.

Milestone status: Shortlisted for submission to the R&D Committee. This is not a notice of final project approval, funding award or completed evaluation.

ICRI-STE recognises this milestone as part of its efforts to encourage early-career research in privacy-preserving AI and computational oncology.
