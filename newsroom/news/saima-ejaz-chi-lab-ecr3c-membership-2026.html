Dr. Saima Ejaz Recognised in the CHI Lab Research Community

ICRI-STE confirms Dr. Saima Ejaz’s ECR-3C classification and complimentary Future Leaders Network membership following review of her academic record and research expertise.

08 September 2026·ECR R&D·ICRI-STE

The International Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE) has confirmed Dr. Saima Ejaz as a member of the Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab Research Community.

In its letter dated 8 September 2026, ICRI-STE records the review of her academic background, research interests and peer-reviewed publications. The areas of expertise identified include pharmaceutical chemistry, computational drug discovery, medicinal chemistry, antibody modelling, molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulations and structure-based drug design.

Membership and research alignment

Her first-author publications were assessed by the CHI Lab Principal Investigator and Founding Director as aligned with the laboratory’s research scope. The letter confirms complimentary membership in the ICRI-STE Future Leaders Network and classification as ECR-3C (PhD in Computational Science & Engineering) under the ICRI-STE ECR Policy.

The correspondence also notes that, after three months in the ECR-3C category and subject to applicable eligibility requirements, she may be considered for progression to Principal Scientist (R3-Scientist). This is a potential future consideration, not a confirmed appointment.
