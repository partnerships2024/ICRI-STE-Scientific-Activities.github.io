Ahmed Ben Khalfa Joins the CHI Lab Research Community as an ECR-1B Member

ICRI-STE confirms Ahmed Ben Khalfa’s complimentary Future Leaders Network membership and ECR-1B classification, recognising his AI/ML engineering background and Dermascope AI research project.

02 October 2026·ECR R&D·ICRI-STE

The International Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE) has confirmed Ahmed Ben Khalfa as a member of the Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab Research Community.

The confirmation letter, dated 2 October 2026, records a review of his academic background in AI/ML Engineering and practical experience in computer vision, deep learning, natural language processing, large and small language model deployment, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines.

Research alignment

His current project, “Dermascope AI: A Multimodal Deep Learning System for Early Melanoma Detection via Feature-wise Linear Modulation,” was identified in the letter as aligned with the CHI Lab’s research scope in AI-driven healthcare and biomedical research.

Based on his BS degree in AI/ML Engineering from the National Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology (INSAT), University of Carthage, Tunisia, his membership is classified as ECR-1B (BS – In Progress) under the ICRI-STE Early Career Researcher policies. The letter also confirms complimentary membership in the ICRI-STE Future Leaders Network.

This membership provides a formal route for engagement with eligible ICRI-STE research, training and community activities, subject to the applicable policies.
