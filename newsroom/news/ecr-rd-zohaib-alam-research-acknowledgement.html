Recognising Zohaib Alam’s Research Contribution

Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab · ICRI-STE

The International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE), through its Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, recognises the research contribution of Zohaib Alam to an interdisciplinary study investigating mutation-centred genomic sequence modelling for lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) stage stratification.

Research contribution

The study, titled “Transcript-Aware Mutation-Centred DNA Reconstruction for Lung Adenocarcinoma Stage Stratification: A Multi-Cohort Deep Learning and Federated Learning Study”, explores transcript-aware mutation reconstruction and deep-learning approaches for distinguishing early-stage (I–II) from advanced-stage (III–IV) LUAD. The work reports experiments using CNN, LSTM and BiLSTM models, alongside a simulated multi-site federated-learning evaluation.

Research affiliation:
Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, International Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE).

Acknowledgement

ICRI-STE acknowledges Zohaib Alam’s contribution to this research activity and encourages continued development through rigorous, collaborative and responsible research.
