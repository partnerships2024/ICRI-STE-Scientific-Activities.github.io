ECR R&D · Research Acknowledgement

Recognising Zohaib Alam’s Research Contribution

Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab · ICRI-STE
✓ PROJECT COMPLETED

The International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE), through its Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, formally acknowledges the research contribution of Zohaib Alam to a completed interdisciplinary research project investigating mutation-centred genomic sequence modelling for lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) stage stratification.

Research Project

“Transcript-Aware Mutation-Centred DNA Reconstruction for Lung Adenocarcinoma Stage Stratification: A Multi-Cohort Deep Learning and Federated Learning Study”

The project explored transcript-aware mutation reconstruction and deep-learning approaches for distinguishing early-stage (I–II) from advanced-stage (III–IV) LUAD. The research incorporated experiments using CNN, LSTM and BiLSTM models, alongside a simulated multi-site federated-learning evaluation.

Project Completion and Leadership

The research project has been successfully completed under the direction and leadership of Dr. Didar Murad, Director of the Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, ICRI-STE. The project formed part of the CHI Lab's interdisciplinary research and development activities in artificial intelligence, computational oncology and precision healthcare.

Project Status: Completed

Acknowledgement

ICRI-STE acknowledges Zohaib Alam's contribution to this completed research activity and recognises the collaborative research environment, scientific guidance and project leadership provided through the CHI Lab. The project represents the Lab's continued commitment to rigorous, collaborative and responsible research in computational healthcare intelligence.
