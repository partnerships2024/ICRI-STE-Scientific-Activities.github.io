Research & Development · Early Career Research Recognition

Recognising Research Contributions of Zohaib Alam

Advancing Computational Oncology through Genomic Modelling, Deep Learning and Federated Learning

Institution: ICRI-STE Research Unit: CHI Lab Project Status: Completed Scientific Leadership: Dr. Didar Murad

The International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE), through its Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, formally acknowledges the research contribution of Zohaib Alam to an interdisciplinary computational oncology research initiative focused on genomic sequence modelling and stage stratification in lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD).

The research project has been completed under the scientific leadership of Dr. Didar Murad, PhD, Principal Scientist and Founding Director of the CHI Lab. The initiative reflects the Lab's commitment to developing early-career research capabilities, advancing computational approaches to complex biomedical problems, and fostering collaborative research at the intersection of mathematical modelling, artificial intelligence and precision medicine.

Research Project and Scientific Scope

"Transcript-Aware Mutation-Centred DNA Reconstruction for Lung Adenocarcinoma Stage Stratification: A Multi-Cohort Deep Learning and Federated Learning Study"

The study investigates computational strategies for integrating transcript-aware mutation-centred genomic reconstruction with deep-learning-based classification to distinguish early-stage (I–II) from advanced-stage (III–IV) lung adenocarcinoma.

Its methodological framework explores the application of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks and Bidirectional LSTM (BiLSTM) architectures, alongside a simulated multi-site federated-learning evaluation. The broader objective is to investigate computational approaches for genomic representation learning and privacy-conscious collaborative analysis across distributed research settings.

Research domain

Computational Oncology · Genomic Sequence Analysis · Deep Learning · Federated Learning · Lung Adenocarcinoma

Research affiliation

Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, International Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE).

Research Contribution and Development

Zohaib Alam's involvement forms part of the CHI Lab's early-career research and development activities, which emphasise the integration of computational methods with clinically relevant biomedical research questions.

The completed research initiative encompasses the following methodological dimensions:

  • Exploration of transcript-aware, mutation-centred genomic sequence reconstruction.
  • Investigation of deep-learning architectures for LUAD stage stratification.
  • Comparative evaluation of CNN, LSTM and BiLSTM modelling approaches.
  • Investigation of simulated federated-learning strategies for multi-cohort genomic analysis.
  • Development of reproducible computational research practices and scientific documentation.
