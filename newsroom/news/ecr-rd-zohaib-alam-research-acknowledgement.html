ECR R&D · Research Acknowledgement

Recognising Zohaib Alam’s Research Contribution

Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab · International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE)
✓ RESEARCH PROJECT COMPLETED

The International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE), through its Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, formally acknowledges Zohaib Alam for his research contribution to a completed interdisciplinary research project investigating mutation-centred genomic sequence reconstruction and deep-learning-based stage stratification in lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD).

Research Project

“Transcript-Validated Mutation-Centred DNA Reconstruction for Lung Adenocarcinoma Stage Stratification: A Centralised and Federated Deep Learning Study”

The project investigated transcript-validated, mutation-centred DNA reconstruction and deep-learning approaches for distinguishing early-stage (I–II) from advanced-stage (III–IV) lung adenocarcinoma. The research incorporated comparative experiments using CNN, LSTM and BiLSTM architectures, together with a simulated multi-site federated-learning framework to investigate the potential of collaborative model training across distributed data environments.

Project Completion and Research Leadership

The research project has been successfully completed under the scientific direction and research leadership of Dr. Didar Murad, Principal Scientist & Founding Director of the Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab, ICRI-STE.

This research forms part of the CHI Lab’s broader interdisciplinary research and development activities in artificial intelligence, computational oncology, biomedical modelling and precision healthcare.
