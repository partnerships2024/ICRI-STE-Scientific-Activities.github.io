Academic and Research Achievements – Dr Didar Murad

Reference date: 01 June 2026 · University of South Wales, Cardiff, UK

The International Council for Research & Innovation in Science, Technology & Engineering (ICRI-STE) records an academic reference concerning the research activities and professional achievements of Dr Didar Murad. The reference is dated 1 June 2026 and is issued by a Professor of Computing in the Faculty of Computing, Engineering and Science at the University of South Wales, Cardiff, UK.

Postdoctoral Research

The reference states that Dr Murad holds a PhD in Computational Science and Engineering and, at the time of the letter, was undertaking postdoctoral research as a Visiting Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare (Cancer Oncology) at the Faculty of Computing, Engineering and Science, University of South Wales.

Interdisciplinary Research Activities

Under the professor’s academic supervision, Dr Murad was described as actively engaged in interdisciplinary research projects spanning artificial intelligence, computational oncology, biomedical modelling and data-driven healthcare applications. The reference highlights his analytical and computational expertise, independent research capabilities, scientific rigour and commitment to high-quality research.

It also recognises his participation in collaborative research initiatives and his ability to address complex biomedical challenges through innovative computational and AI-driven methodologies.

Professional Recommendation

In the reference, the professor recommends Dr Murad for academic appointments and research-oriented positions in academia and industry. The letter expresses confidence that he will contribute to scientific advancement, interdisciplinary collaboration and institutional research excellence.
