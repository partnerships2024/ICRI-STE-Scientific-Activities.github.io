Saddia Bano Recognised as an ECR-2C Member of the CHI Lab Community

An archived ICRI-STE membership confirmation recognises Saddia Bano’s bioinformatics background and experience in genomic and transcriptomic data analysis.

05 June 2025·ECR R&D·ICRI-STE

In a membership confirmation dated 5 June 2025, the International Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE) recorded Saddia Bano as a member of the Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab Research Community.

The letter identifies her core expertise as genomic and transcriptomic data analysis, biological data processing and interpretation, and next-generation sequencing (NGS).

Early Career Researcher classification

The correspondence classifies her membership as ECR-2C (MS-Completed) under the ICRI-STE Early Career Researcher policies and confirms access to eligible research, training and community activities in accordance with applicable ICRI-STE policies.

This item is retained as an archival record of the CHI Lab’s early research-community development.

The date shown here is the date printed on the letter. Its reference number appears to use a different year; the newsroom therefore does not reproduce that reference number.
