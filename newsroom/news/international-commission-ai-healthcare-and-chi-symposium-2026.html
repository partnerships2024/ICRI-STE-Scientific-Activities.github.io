ICRI-STE Invites Prof. Andrew Ware to Contribute to AI in Healthcare Commission

ICRI-STE has invited Prof. Dr Andrew Ware to serve in a voluntary advisory and research-evaluation capacity for the CHI Lab’s AI in Healthcare activities during 2026–2027, and to consider a keynote contribution to its first virtual symposium.

01 October 2026·Institutional Announcements·ICRI-STE

The International Council for Research & Innovation in STE (ICRI-STE) has formally invited Prof. Dr Andrew Ware to serve as a Commission Member for AI in Healthcare within its Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab.

As outlined in the invitation dated 1 October 2026, the proposed voluntary role would bring his academic expertise to the review and evaluation of research and innovation activities involving CHI Lab scientists and Early Career Researchers (ECRs) across the ECR-1 (BS), ECR-2 (MS) and ECR-3 (PhD) levels during 2026–2027.

Supporting research quality and early-career development

The proposed commission is intended to provide professional perspectives that support research quality, interdisciplinary collaboration and the development of CHI Lab researchers. The invitation also asks Prof. Ware to consider joining as a keynote speaker at the 1st Virtual International Symposium of CHI Lab Scientists & Early Career Researchers 2026.

Status: This announcement reports an invitation issued by ICRI-STE. It does not imply that the invitation has been accepted or that an appointment has been confirmed.

ICRI-STE values international academic engagement as part of its efforts to strengthen responsible, collaborative and high-quality research in AI in Healthcare.
