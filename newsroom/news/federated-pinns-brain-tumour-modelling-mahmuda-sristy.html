Privacy-Preserving AI

Federated PINNs for Privacy-Preserving Brain Tumour Biomechanical Modelling

ICRI-STE recognises the research alignment of Mahmuda Sristy’s publication on federated physics-informed neural networks for privacy-preserving brain tumour biomechanical modelling.

10 August 2026·Research Highlights·ICRI-STE

The CHI Lab has identified the publication “Where Physics Meets Privacy: Federated PINNs for Privacy-Preserving Brain Tumor Biomechanical Modeling” by Mahmuda Sristy as aligned with its research scope.

The ICRI-STE membership letter dated 10 August 2026 records an assessment of her academic background in Computer Science and Engineering, research interests and publications. Her stated interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, large language models and multimodal AI.

Research relevance

The publication’s title brings together two important themes for computational healthcare research: physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for biomechanical modelling and federated approaches intended to support privacy-preserving computation.

Based on her BS degree in Computer Science and Engineering from United International University (UIU), Bangladesh, ICRI-STE classified her membership as ECR-1C (BS-CS Completed) and confirmed complimentary membership in the ICRI-STE Future Leaders Network.

The letter confirms that the publication was assessed as aligned with the CHI Lab’s research scope. It does not constitute a separate endorsement of the publication’s findings.
