Healthcare · Biomedical AI

Computational Healthcare Intelligence (CHI) Lab

Advancing computational healthcare, biomedical artificial intelligence, computational oncology, systems biology, medical data science, and trustworthy intelligent healthcare. Research spans biomedical modelling, medical imaging, transcriptomics, federated learning, and agentic research platforms.

Environment · Autonomous systems

Environmental Intelligence & Autonomous Systems (EIAS) Lab
